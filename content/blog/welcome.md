Welcome! We're excited to bring Japanese Pokémon TCG sealed products to collectors, starting right here in the Bay Area.

## Why Japanese Pokémon Products?

After spending over a year selling at local Bay Area card shows and vendor events, I kept getting questions about Japanese Pokémon products and singles. Those conversations sparked the idea to start offering Japanese sealed products, and one thing led to another.

Now, I'm excited to build on that experience and bring more Japanese Pokémon TCG products to collectors.

## How We Source

We're fortunate to work with reliable suppliers in Japan to source our products. Our goal is to make it easier for collectors to find Japanese sealed products they're looking for.

## What's Next?

We're starting with Japanese sealed booster boxes, with plans to expand our selection as the shop grows.

Looking for something specific? Feel free to [contact us](/#contact) with special requests for Japanese sealed products, including items like Chinese Gem Pack boxes printed in Japan. We'll do our best to help you track them down.

Want to hear about new arrivals? [Sign up for restock alerts](/#join) to be among the first to hear about upcoming drops.

Thanks for being here and supporting the shop. We're excited to see where this goes!
